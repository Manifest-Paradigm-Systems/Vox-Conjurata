The package `documents` contains modules: `errors.py` (defines exception tree), `guards.py` (defines `check_prefix` returning `PrefixCheck` and `assert_prefix` taking two byte strings), `byterange.py` (defines byte range utilities), `incremental.py` (defines `append_bytes` and `append_to_file`), `fill.py` (defines `fill_form`), `overlay.py` (defines `overlay_text`), `sigfield.py` (defines `append_signature_field`), `widget.py` (defines `append_signature_widget`), `report.py` (defines `ChangeReport`, `SignReport`, `SignatureFieldReport`), `pdf_info.py` (defines `describe_pdf`), `forms.py` (defines `known_fields`), `coords.py` (defines coordinate conversion), `locate.py` (defines `find_blank_regions`), `cms.py` (defines `CardSigner`), `sign.py` (defines `sign_pdf`), `validate.py` (defines `validate_signatures`), `receipt.py` (defines receipt generation), `prepare.py` (defines `prepare_for_signature`), `cli.py` (defines command-line interface), and `__init__.py` (lazy facade). The `signers` package holds the signing seam: `__init__.py` defines the `RawSigner` and `Pkcs11Session` protocols, `signer_from_env()` and `doctor()`; `software.py`, `fake.py`, `pkcs11_session.py` and `pkcs11_python.py` hold the implementations. All functions use `pathlib.Path` for paths, and `PrefixCheck` is a simple object with an `ok` attribute. The prefix invariant is enforced via `documents.guards.assert_prefix` (two arguments) and byte range consistency via `documents.byterange.assert_byterange_consistent` (two arguments).
